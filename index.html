Office Days Calculator

Find your minimum in-office days for a month using the hybrid policy and your calendar.

Your local data file

Office days, holidays, leave and approved exceptions load from office-days-data.json in the HybridCalculator folder and are saved there after every change. Only the current and previous year's records are retained. No database or browser storage is used. Double-click Open Calculator.cmd to start the app and open your browser. Keep its window open while using the app.
